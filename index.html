Learn AI Academia with Phoebe

AI for the people who cannot afford to be wrong in print

Researchers, PhD students and professors are handed the boldest claims about AI - it will do your literature review, write your grant, analyse your data - and carry the highest cost when those claims fail: a fabricated citation with your name above it, a silently biased screen, a funder ruling your ideas were not yours. This course is the no-bluff version. A leader track gives PIs, professors and lab heads the policy landscape as it actually stands - the NIH notices, the publisher rules, ICMJE and COPE, authorship, supervision and the lab AI policy worth writing. A researcher track runs the full cycle hands-on: search, screening, extraction, hypothesis discipline, research code with tests, analysis, writing with honest disclosure, and peer review. At its centre, a live screening lab: 40 records, 12 truly relevant, recall computed before your eyes - climbing 4 to 11 of 12 as real practices come on, and falling back the moment you let AI screen unverified. Every policy claim on these pages traces to a primary source, and everything a tool is said to do traces to its documentation or a peer-reviewed evaluation.
