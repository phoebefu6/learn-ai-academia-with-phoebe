# Learn AI Academia with Phoebe

AI for the people who cannot afford to be wrong in print - researchers, PhD students and
professors. Two tracks, 16 sessions, no bluff: every policy claim traces to a primary source,
every tool claim to vendor documentation or a peer-reviewed evaluation.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-ai-academia-with-phoebe/

## What is inside

- **Leader track (a1-a6)** - for PIs, professors, lab heads and research-office leaders: the
  failure catalogue with retraction notices attached, the policy rails (NIH, NSF, publishers,
  ICMJE, COPE) from primary sources, supervision and authorship, grants, peer review duty, and
  a one-page lab AI policy drafted in session.
- **Researcher track (b1-b10)** - for PhD students, postdocs and hands-on researchers: the full
  research cycle with AI in its honest place - search, screening, extraction, hypothesis
  discipline, research code with tests, analysis without forking paths, writing with clean
  disclosure, and peer review within the rules.
- **The screening lab** (`assets/acad-live.js`) - a live systematic-review screen: 40 records,
  12 truly relevant, recall and precision computed live as five practices toggle on - and an
  anti-lever that shows what unverified AI screening silently costs.

## Format

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. 45-minute sessions: concepts as Live/Self-study cards,
build-along demos on your own research (never toy exercises), a 3-question quiz, honest
source-coverage rows, and a cheat sheet per session.

Sources verified August 2026 - the course map (`materials/official-course-map.md`) carries the
full primary-source appendix and the list of claims deliberately flagged unverified.
